About MahaRide

MahaRide connects travellers with rental vehicles and promotes tourism across Maharashtra. Our tagline says it: Ride Maharashtra. Discover More.

This is a free prototype built as a college Community Engagement Project. It uses sample data and your browser’s local storage. No real bookings or payments are made.

Team: Tushar Pawar (59), Anubhav Thombare (75), Harshad Walhekar (79), Rutik Raut (61). Guide: Prof. Priti Dhotre.
